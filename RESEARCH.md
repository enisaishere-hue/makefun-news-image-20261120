# Research notes — 20 November 2026

All seven stories were checked against sources first published or updated
on 20 November 2026 (UTC). Figures are quoted as the sources reported
them; where sources disagree, the poster uses the most conservative wording
and the note below says so.

## 1. GTA VI — hero story

- Reuters, 19 Nov, updated 20 Nov 00:03:49Z: Sensor Tower estimates
  24.58 million units and about $2.36 billion as of Thursday evening —
  roughly three times GTA V's $800 million first day.
- Deadline, published 2026-11-20T00:38:28Z (via AmeriLedger): $2.36
  billion, 24.59 million units, 75% of sales on PlayStation.
- Caution: these are Sensor Tower estimates, not official Take-Two or
  Rockstar figures. The poster says "estimated" twice and the footer
  repeats it.

## 2. Trump, Xi and Putin meet in Shenzhen

- BBC, 20 Nov: the three leaders held a first-ever three-way meeting in
  Shenzhen on 19 Nov and released a joint "peace declaration". The agenda
  covered nuclear arms control, Iran and the Russia-Ukraine war.
- Corroborated by multiple 20 Nov headline round-ups (Times of India,
  school-assembly wires) describing the same meeting.

## 3. Cyclone Akvan

- India Meteorological Department: landfall near Gopalpur around noon IST
  on Friday 20 Nov (06:30 GMT), sustained winds 90–100 km/h, gusts to
  110 km/h.
- Evacuation counts conflict across sources (about 105,000 by Thursday
  afternoon per Odisha Chief Secretary Anu Garg; over 300,000 per The
  Daily Jagran on 20 Nov, with 423 rescue teams and 69 trains cancelled),
  so the poster says only "hundreds of thousands evacuated" and keeps the
  423 rescue-team figure, which is consistent across reports.

## 4. Taiz, Yemen

- BBC, published 2026-11-20T00:13:21Z, Orla Guerin: seven people inside
  the besieged city describe life under siege; 148 civilians killed by
  Houthi attacks since August, per the Office for Siege Affairs.

## 5. Cricket — New Zealand vs India, Wellington

- Reuters/AP, published 2026-11-20T00:20:03–00:22:56Z: Glenn Phillips
  scored 118 — the first batter with centuries against India in all three
  formats in one calendar year; New Zealand 317-6 at lunch on day two,
  Henry Nicholls 146 not out.

## 6. Gold

- Wall Street Journal, 20 Nov: front-month gold fell 1.5% to $3,682.10
  an ounce, its lowest since 19 September 2025; silver fell to a 14-month
  low. The slide followed growing bets on a December Fed rate hike
  (CME FedWatch: ~90% chance of a 25 bp hike).
- Reuters, 19 Nov: spot gold had touched $3,693.13 earlier in the
  session, its lowest since September 2025. The poster uses the WSJ
  $3,682 figure because it is the 20 Nov close.

## 7. Ramayana — day 15 box office

- Box Office India (confirmed figures, updated 20 Nov): day 15
  (Thu 19 Nov) ₹19.00 crore net in India; cumulative India net ₹851.10
  crore, India gross ₹1,015.64 crore, overseas ₹514.96 crore, worldwide
  gross ₹1,530.60 crore.
- Early estimates from other trackers ranged ₹19.00–19.35 crore for the
  day; the poster rounds to "about ₹19 crore" and "₹851 crore /
  ₹1,531 crore" to stay inside the confirmed range.
